Ricki DiCristoforo

Former software developer, future librarian, life-long book lover.

About Ricki

After working as a software engineer for nearly eight years, Ricki decided to shake up her career and become a librarian. She would like to work in the public library sphere, to help encourage a love of reading and support social programs within her community in Washington DC. Ricki loves exploring National Parks (10 and counting!), crocheting, and spoiling her four cats.

Education

Ricki graduated Tulane University in 2018 with a double major in Computer Science and Linguistics. She is currently pursuing a Master's of Library Science at Pennsylvania Western University.

Past Experience

Ricki has worked as a web developer, a full stack developer, and a backend developer.
